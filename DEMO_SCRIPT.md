**Target length:** 2:15–2:45 (sweet spot). Optional 3:30 cut at the end.  
**Record:** screen capture of http://localhost:3847 (or hosted URL) + your voice.  
**Prep once before rolling:** Reset demo fill → Find the bridge → wait for results → leave that tab ready. For take 2 you can re-run live if rate limits are cool.

## Voice-over (main cut ~2:30)

### [0:00 — cold open. Hero on screen. Brand “Truce” big.]

Two tables. Two media diets. Same country.  
They don’t need another debate. They need something they already share — that isn’t politics.

This is **Truce**.

### [0:18 — show Blue table / Red table with sections]

Each side is a taste table you can edit: media, movies, artists, food, holidays.  
Demo fill is loaded — MSNBC, Times, NPR on one side; Fox, Journal, Blaze on the other — plus a bit of film, food, and travel for colour.

### [0:35 — briefly edit: remove one chip or add “Austin”]

It’s not a black box form. You can change the tables.  
But here’s the important bit from building on Qloo: if you feed opposing *artists* in as signals, the graphs pull apart.  
So the **bridge is computed from the media diets**. Movies and artists on the card are context. Honesty over theatre.

### [0:55 — click Find the bridge]

Find the bridge.  
One live call path into Qloo Insights — entity by entity, steady, because parallel bursts hit rate limits.  
We’re asking: given these cultural signals, what do both sides already lean toward?

### [1:15 — results: agenda]

Here’s tonight’s truce agenda — three concrete moves. Not “be civil.” Put something on.  
Memory prompts, not critique. Who showed you this. What car you were in. Defenses drop faster than arguments.

### [1:30 — Venn]

Simple map. Left only. Shared. Right only.  
Real entity names from this run. No fake “sixty percent overlap” charts.  
The middle is the point.

### [1:42 — scroll bridge list: artist + movie if visible]

Each bridge card has affinity from both sides, a why, a **Try asking** line, and a low-stakes **table game** — Blind Pick a track, Watch Swap twenty minutes, phones down or you do the dishes.

### [2:00 — Open Truce Deck]

If the list feels long for a dinner, open **Truce Deck**.  
Same bridges, one card at a time. Left and Right each tap Love or Hard Pass.

### [2:10 — both Love → Common ground locked]

When both love it — common ground locked.  
That’s the product moment: shared culture, confirmed by people at the table, grounded in Qloo’s graph.

### [2:22 — Back to list / click Truce logo]

Truce doesn’t invent the middle of the aisle.  
It finds the song, the film, the place both graphs already knew — and gives the table something human to do next.

Built for the Qloo Agentic Hackathon. Agents, but with taste.

**Truce.**
